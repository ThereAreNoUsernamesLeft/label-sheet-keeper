Label Sheet Keeper for Word

A Word side panel that remembers which labels on a sheet are used, so you can finish partly used sheets.

Set it up on your Mac (once)

  1. Download manifest.xml
  2. Quit Word.
  3. In Finder, press ⌘⇧G and go to ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/
  4. If there's no folder named wef, make one (File › New Folder).
  5. Put manifest.xml inside wef.
  6. Open Word and any document. Click Label Sheet on the Home tab. If it isn't there, click Add-ins on the Home tab and choose Label Sheet Keeper.

Use it

  1. In the panel, pick a size and click Make a new label sheet. Save the new document.
  2. Open the panel in that document and click the spots already peeled off your sheet.
  3. Type a label, leave the cursor in it, and click Copy to next open.
  4. Print with ⌘P at 100% scale. Then click I printed these and save.

On shipping label sheets, the panel can also put an Amazon return label PDF (or any label picture) on the next open label, or lay out a From / Ship to address label for you.

Label sizes

  • 1″ × 2⅝″, 30 per sheet (fits Avery 5160 / 8160). Blank sheet
  • Shipping labels, 2 per sheet, shown as a sideways page with two upright 5½″ × 8½″ labels (fits Avery 5126 / 8126). Blank sheet

To add a size, edit formats.js in the GitHub repository. Instructions are at the top of that file.
