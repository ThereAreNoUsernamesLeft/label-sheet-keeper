This panel runs inside Microsoft Word. Open Word and click Label Sheet on the Home tab.

Setup steps are on the Label Sheet Keeper page.

This version of Word is too old for the panel. Update Word (Help › Check for Updates), then reopen it.

Make a label sheet

Choose the labels you have. The sheet opens as a new Word document laid out to match them.

Nothing opened?

Download a blank sheet and open it in Word instead:

1Mark used labels

Click each label on the map that’s already peeled off your sheet. Click again to make it open.

2Fill labels

Type a label in the document and leave the cursor in it. Then copy it to open labels.

2Add a shipping label

An Amazon return label or any carrier label, as a PDF or a picture. You can also drop the file here.

Drag across the label to leave out instructions or a packing slip.
