Label Sheet Keeper

Send a label

Choose a return label: a PDF or a screenshot. It shows up in the Word panel, ready to print.

Send from the Share button
  1. Copy your sending address.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name the shortcut Send to Label Sheet Keeper.
  3. Tap the Details button (ⓘ), turn on Show in Share Sheet, and tap Done.
  4. Add the action Get Contents of URL and paste the address as its URL.
  5. Tap Show More. Set Method to POST and Request Body to File, then choose Shortcut Input as the file.
  6. Add the action Show Notification and set its text to Contents of URL.

Then, on a label in Mail, Files or Photos, tap Share › Send to Label Sheet Keeper.

Keep this page on your Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Labels wait on the Label Sheet Keeper server until the panel picks them up, for 7 days at most. Keep your sending address to yourself.
